# Boston Initials 1889

Native JP2 crops are recorded in tracing.json. No missing initial letters are invented; ordinary sample text and numerals are excluded. Size variants remain separate historical impressions. Repeated letters within a combination demonstration are documented in inventory.json.

Local paper correction uses a 65-pixel maximum filter (radius 32) to reach paper around the broad initial strokes. It does not add strokes or close gaps. Recorded thresholds produce the monochrome drawing and disjoint three-tone regions; the tones interpret scanned ink strength rather than separately printed colours.

The longest ink dimension fits 1800 units in a 2048-unit em, with 64-unit sidebearings and uniform scaling. Source aspect ratios are preserved. Potrace 1.16 prepares outlines for the canonical CFF TTX master; standard builds require no network or tracing tool.

Acquisition and rights evidence are retained under reference/. The source book is the 1889 Boston Type Foundry specimen; original revival work is copyright Harold Lehmann under the repository MIT license.

The 312 exports cover printed pp. 237–241. Six repeated demonstration letters are documented separately. Series 11 and 12 each print 25 capitals without a separate J. Page 240 does not show parts 24 and 25; page 241 places part 34 earlier in its layout. The inventories preserve these distinctions and do not synthesise missing letters or components.

Final close review removes detached paper specks beyond the flourishes of Series 12 L and Series 15 V. Explicit crop-relative erasure rectangles are recorded; nearby engraved strokes are retained.
