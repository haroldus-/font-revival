# Ryan 1894 and The Inland Printer, November 1888

Survey completed 18 September 2026 from the two requested Archive items:
[John Ryan Foundry, *Latest and Standard Faces in Type*](https://archive.org/details/lateststandardfa00ryan)
and [*The Inland Printer*, vol. 6, no. 2](https://archive.org/details/sim_american-printer_1888-11_6_2).
The second item's modern catalogue title is "Inland Printer/American Lithographer";
the historical pages are headed *The Inland Printer*.

OCR served as an index, followed by visual survey sheets and full-resolution
inspection of shortlisted pages. The review concentrated on named type specimens,
particularly Ryan's display section, printed pp. 59–148, and the journal's foundry
advertisements. Body faces, scripts, advertising lettering, borders and machinery
were also screened. This is a selection for this collection, not a claim that every
typeface in the volumes is identified or has never been digitised elsewhere.

## Selected and completed

| Face | Primary pages | Why select it | Evidence limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Trinal 1888](../collection/trinal-1888/) | Inland Printer 138–139; Ryan 130–131; patent D18,671 | Named showings in both requested books, attributable designer and patent alphabet, distinctive curled capitals, usable large impressions | The design changes with size. One master follows the 24-point design with documented supplements, not the complete suite of initials and optical variants. |
| [Quaint Gothic 1894](../collection/quaint-gothic-1894/) | Ryan 148 | Strong contrast with the existing collection, clear large impressions and repeated letters, intentional long descenders | Q, Z and four figures are missing and explicitly inferred. The source does not name a designer. The year identifies the specimen. |

Both releases are capitals-only. Lowercase input maps to capitals. Their local
source records, exact reference pages, crop measurements, companion drawings,
initial preparation recipes, editable TTX masters, source-comparison PDFs and
desktop/web outputs are committed together. See [TRACING.md](TRACING.md).

## Further prospects

| Priority | Face and printed page | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | **Gretchen**, Inland Printer 140 | An attractive condensed, mixed-case face from Lindsay Type Foundry. Several sizes and useful running text, but a sparse capital alphabet. Seek the patent announced as pending or a fuller Lindsay specimen before drawing. |
| High | **Crusader**, Ryan 104 | Strong asymmetric capitals, open spacing and characteristic A/M forms. Multiple sizes offer useful comparison. A complete alphabet would reduce inference of rare capitals. |
| High | **Marine**, Ryan 100 | A lively, rounded mixed-case design that would broaden the collection. Several sizes, but incomplete letter coverage; seek a full historical alphabet. |
| Medium | **Erebus / Hades**, Ryan 76–77 | A compelling paired silhouette/outline optical effect. The catalogue explicitly describes overprinting the pair. Best treated as a coordinated two-font project, requiring consistent registration and fuller alphabets. |
| Medium | **Oriole**, Ryan 107 | Distinctive incised ornamental capitals. Fine detail needs a sharper supplementary impression and a complete alphabet before faithful reconstruction. |
| Medium | **Grady**, Ryan 133 | Strong condensed display forms with mixed case. Useful samples, but less contrast with existing Nero and missing letters that should be researched first. |
| Medium | **Jagged**, Inland Printer 147; Ryan 121 | Unusual angular designs. Ryan distinguishes three numbered versions and separate Outing faces; avoid combining them. Current journal samples are small. |
| Later | **Remington**, Inland Printer 137 | Extensive running text makes lowercase well evidenced. Few capitals and small impressions; a better typewriter-type specimen would strengthen it. |
| Already represented | **Nero**, Inland Printer 141 / Ryan 135; **Johnson**, Ryan 145 | Existing families in the repository; avoid duplicating them. |

"Priority" reflects the combination of design value, evidence, legibility of the
available scans and variety added to this repository. It is not a historical ranking
or a statement about modern commercial availability.

## Research cautions and rights

The secondary [Roberts/Saxe patent index](https://www.galleyrack.com/images/artifice/letters/press/typemaking/history/typeface-index/roberts-saxe/typeface-patent-database.pdf)
lists Trinal with D18,516. Checking the primary documents established
[D18,671, dated 9 October 1888](https://patents.google.com/patent/USD18671S/en),
whose plate matches the advertisement. The mistaken number was not propagated.

Searches for "Quaint Gothic" also return material about a different design, often
called "Quaint" or "Desdemona". Ryan prints **Quaint** on p. 74 and **Quaint Gothic**
on p. 148 as visibly different faces. No designer attribution was transferred from
those search results, and no modern digital outlines were used.

Ryan's Archive metadata credits the Library of Congress and records its statement
that it is unaware of copyright restrictions. Extracts are retained with both new
families. The journal's date, issue and microfilm-source metadata are retained with
Trinal. Both are nineteenth-century US publications; the public-domain term basis
is [US Copyright Office Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf).
The patent PDF and its seven-year grant are also retained. Exact URLs and page
numbers are in each `font.json`; historical source material retains its status.
